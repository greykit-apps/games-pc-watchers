# PumaCity: Watchers

**An alternate-universe Casey Benton runs a dawn-of-cyberpunk sprawl with an iron paw.** Manage the city while building an elite team of enforcers to push out a new rival. A squad tactics game heavily inspired by Bullfrog's *Syndicate* (1993).

**Play:** [greykit.com/games/watchers](https://greykit.com/games/watchers/)

![PumaCity: Watchers. An alternate-universe Casey Benton runs a dawn-of-cyberpunk sprawl with an iron paw.](og.png)

## How it plays

You run the **Watchers**, Casey Benton's enforcers, holding her city of **eight zones** against **Cal**, a rival pushing in one zone at a time. On the command screens you set each zone's tax, split the take between research and money, buy weapons in the Armoury and cyber-mods in the Clinic, hire Watchers and pick missions. Each mission shows its threat; take on more than the squad can handle and Watchers are lost for good.

On a mission you lead four Watchers through one district in real time: a hit, a talk-round, a theft, a siege. Walk in with guns away and nobody looks twice; draw, and everyone does. Then get everyone still standing to the getaway car. Once the research is in, **Take Control**: every zone gets a story to finish, and locking down all eight wins the game. The full rules are in [docs/gameplay.md](docs/gameplay.md).

## Keys

A desktop game for keyboard and mouse (the right button is used constantly), in a window about 1024 pixels wide or more. `?` at any time explains how it works; Help on the title screen has the full controls. `M` is sound.

| On a mission | |
|---|---|
| Left click | move, or select a Watcher; drag to select several |
| Right click | fire; with the Purrsuader out, talk someone round |
| `1`–`4`, `` ` `` | select a Watcher, or the whole squad |
| `E` / `C` | go loud / stay calm |
| `R` / `Q` | guns out or away / the Purrsuader out or away |
| `F` | first aid |
| `WASD`, arrows | scroll the view |
| `Space` / `Esc` | pause / menu |

On the command screens, `1`–`5` pick the City, the Office, the Armoury, the Clinic and the Lab; `W` waits a day and `B` briefs the marked mission.

## Saves

The campaign saves itself after every change; *Resume campaign* on the title screen picks it up. Kept in the browser's `localStorage` under keys starting `pcwatchers_`. **Settings → Forget everything** clears them.
